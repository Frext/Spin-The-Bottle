# Spin The Bottle

A simple Android application designed to replicate the classic party game. 

Spin the bottle digitally, switch up your styles on the fly, and let fate decide who's next!

---

## 🍾 How It Works

The application provides an interactive bottle-spinning experience with built-in customization:

1. **Spin the Bottle:** Tap the screen to spin the bottle. It will rotate dynamically and slow down to point randomly.
2. **Switch Bottle Styles:** Click the three-dot options menu in the top-right corner to open the selection dropdown.
3. **Water Bottle Mode:** Select **"Select the water bottle"** to switch to a modern, clean plastic water bottle graphic.
4. **Classic Green Bottle Mode:** Select **"Select the green bottle"** to toggle back to the classic green glass bottle graphic.
